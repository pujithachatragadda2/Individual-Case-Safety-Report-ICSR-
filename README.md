### 1\. Abstract

Pharmacovigilance is an essential activity in healthcare that focuses on the detection, assessment, understanding and prevention of adverse drug reactions (ADRs).

An Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) is a structured report containing information about a suspected adverse event experienced by an individual patient following exposure to one or more medicinal products.

The preparation of an ICSR involves patient information collection, identification of suspected medicines, documentation of adverse reactions, seriousness assessment, causality assessment and clinical classification.

This project focuses on the complete evaluation of a suspected adverse drug reaction through an individual case safety report. It includes ADR identification, patient and drug information, clinical assessment, Naranjo causality assessment, severity classification, seriousness assessment and expectedness evaluation.

### 2\. Introduction

Medicines are used to prevent, diagnose and treat various diseases. However, their use may sometimes produce unwanted and harmful effects known as adverse drug reactions.

Pharmacovigilance helps healthcare professionals identify and monitor these reactions to ensure the safe use of medicines.

An Individual Case Safety Report is an important pharmacovigilance document used to record and communicate suspected adverse drug reactions associated with medicinal products.

An ICSR contains essential information about:

* Patient demographics.

* Medical history.

* Suspected medicinal products.

* Adverse drug reactions.

* Reaction onset and duration.

* Concomitant medications.

* Laboratory investigations.

* Treatment provided.

* Clinical outcome.

* Causality assessment.

* Seriousness and severity classification.

Proper documentation of ICSRs helps identify potential safety signals and improve medication safety.

### 5\. Overview of Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR)

An Individual Case Safety Report is a standardised report that documents a suspected adverse event associated with the use of a medicinal product in an individual patient.

### Minimum criteria for a valid ICSR

Four essential elements are required:

* An identifiable patient.

* An identifiable reporter.

* A suspected medicinal product.

* A suspected adverse reaction or event.

### Importance of ICSR

* Supports the monitoring of drug safety.

* Helps identify previously unrecognised adverse reactions.

* Facilitates pharmacovigilance data collection.

* Supports signal detection.

* Improves medicine safety monitoring.

* Helps prevent avoidable drug-related harm.

### 6\. Components of an Individual Case Safety Report

### A. Patient Information

* Patient identification number.

* Age.

* Gender.

* Weight.

* Medical history.

* Existing medical conditions.

### B. Reporter Information

* Name or identification of the reporting healthcare professional.

* Professional qualification.

* Healthcare institution.

* Contact information.

### C. Suspected Drug Information

* Generic drug name.

* Brand name.

* Dosage.

* Route of administration.

* Frequency.

* Treatment initiation date.

* Treatment discontinuation date.

* Indication for treatment.

### D. Adverse Reaction Information

* Description of the reaction.

* Date of onset.

* Duration.

* Clinical manifestations.

* Laboratory findings.

* Treatment provided.

* Outcome.

### E. Concomitant Medication Information

Documentation of other medicines taken by the patient during the suspected reaction period.

### F. Causality Assessment

Evaluation of the relationship between the suspected medicine and the adverse reaction.

### G. Seriousness and Severity Classification

Assessment of the clinical intensity and regulatory seriousness of the adverse reaction.

### H. Expectedness Assessment

Comparison of the reported reaction with the established reference safety information of the suspected medicine.

### Patient History

A 58-year-old male patient was prescribed ibuprofen 400 mg three times daily for pain management.

On the fifth day of treatment, the patient developed abdominal discomfort and black-coloured stools (melena).

The patient was admitted to the hospital for further evaluation. Laboratory investigations showed a decrease in haemoglobin levels.

Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy revealed a bleeding gastric ulcer.

Ibuprofen was discontinued, and the patient received appropriate hospital treatment, including proton pump inhibitor therapy.

The patient's clinical condition improved following treatment.

The following case information is hypothetical and intended for academic assessment.

| Route                    | Oral                      |

| Drug action              | Cyclooxygenase inhibition |

### Mechanism of Adverse Reaction

Ibuprofen inhibits cyclooxygenase enzymes COX-1 and COX-2, resulting in decreased prostaglandin synthesis.

Prostaglandins normally protect the gastric mucosa by:

* Increasing mucus secretion.

* Increasing bicarbonate secretion.

* Maintaining gastric mucosal blood flow.

* Supporting mucosal protection.

Inhibition of prostaglandin synthesis reduces the protective mechanisms of the stomach.

This can lead to gastric mucosal damage, ulcer formation and gastrointestinal bleeding.

### 9\. Adverse Drug Reaction Identification

The patient developed the following clinical manifestations:

* Abdominal discomfort.

* Black-coloured stools (melena).

* Decreased haemoglobin levels.

* Endoscopic evidence of a bleeding gastric ulcer.

These findings indicate gastrointestinal bleeding following ibuprofen exposure.

The temporal relationship between drug administration and reaction onset supports a suspected association.

### 12\. Causality Assessment Using the Naranjo Algorithm

The Naranjo Algorithm is a standardised method consisting of ten questions used to evaluate the probability that a suspected drug caused an adverse reaction.

### Assessment of the case

| Naranjo question                                   | Answer        | Score |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ----- |
| Previous conclusive reports of the reaction?       | Yes           | +1    |
| Did the reaction occur after drug administration?  | Yes           | +2    |
| Did the reaction improve after discontinuation?    | Yes           | +1    |
| Did the reaction reappear after re-administration? | No            | -1    |
| Are there alternative causes?                      | No identified | +2    |
| Did the reaction reappear after placebo?           | Unknown       | 0     |
| Was toxic drug concentration detected?             | Unknown       | 0     |
| Was there a dose-response relationship?            | Unknown       | 0     |
| Previous similar reaction?                         | No            | -1    |
| Was the reaction objectively confirmed?            | Yes           | +1    |
| Total score                                        |               | 6     |

### Naranjo Classification

| Score      | Classification |
| ---------- | -------------- |
| 9 or above | Definite       |
| 5–8        | Probable       |
| 1–4        | Possible       |
| 0 or below | Doubtful       |

Probable

Interpretation: The Naranjo score of 6 indicates a probable association between ibuprofen and gastrointestinal bleeding in this hypothetical case.

### 13\. WHO–UMC Causality Assessment

The WHO–UMC system evaluates suspected adverse reactions using clinical judgement.

### Assessment

* Temporal relationship: Reasonable.

* Dechallenge: Improvement following discontinuation.

* Alternative causes: No alternative cause identified in the hypothetical case assessment.

* Clinical evidence: Gastrointestinal bleeding confirmed by endoscopy.

* Rechallenge: Not performed and not required.

Final classification: Probable/Likely.

The classification is based on the stated hypothetical clinical findings and would require verification against a complete patient record in actual clinical practice.

### 14\. Severity Classification

Severity refers to the intensity of the adverse drug reaction.

The gastrointestinal bleeding in this case is classified as severe because it involves significant clinical manifestations, a decrease in haemoglobin and the need for hospital treatment.

### Severity categories

| Category | Description                                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Mild     | Minor symptoms with minimal clinical impact                              |
| Moderate | Noticeable symptoms requiring medical intervention                       |
| Severe   | Intense clinical manifestations requiring significant medical management |

### 15\. Seriousness Classification

The case is classified as serious because the patient required hospitalisation.

Seriousness criteria include:

* Death.

* Life-threatening condition.

* Hospitalisation or prolonged hospitalisation.

* Persistent or significant disability.

* Congenital anomaly.

* Other medically important conditions.

The patient meets the hospitalisation criterion.

### 16\. Expectedness Assessment

Expectedness is assessed by comparing the reported reaction with the applicable reference safety information for the suspected medicine.

Ibuprofen is associated with gastrointestinal ulceration and bleeding.

Therefore, gastrointestinal bleeding is classified as expected, provided the reaction is documented in the applicable approved product information.
